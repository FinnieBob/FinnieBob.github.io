Simulation & Training

Real-time simulation, wargaming, command-and-control and learning environments developed for training and operational use.

CADUCEUS · EW Training & Simulation Suite

Developed in conjunction with the Royal School of Signals during my time at Cranfield University. The system used the SPECTRA/THISTLE project to emulate electronic-warfare transmissions for student exercises. Students deployed assets, tested communications locations, ran scenarios in real time and collaboratively built an operational picture from detections.

My contribution: Software development and simulation engineering.
Use: Training environment for students and instructors.
Technical focus: scenario behaviour, real-time simulation and collaborative information handling.

Project THISTLE · Command & Control Software Suite
British MoD / NATO C3A

Lead developer and engineer on a broad suite of C3, communications-planning and wargaming software tools developed between 1994 and 2010. The systems were used extensively in training and military exercises and deployed live in theatre, including Bosnia and Afghanistan.

My contribution: Lead software developer / engineer.
Technical focus: C2 systems, rapid prototyping, GIS, simulation and high-reliability software.

R&D / Tools

Technical experiments and research projects spanning computer vision, machine learning, signal processing and interactive tools.

Realtime 3D Pose Estimation & Mapping

Unity and Barracuda Neural Network inference used for realtime pose estimation from 2D video, transforming the resulting skeleton into a 3D model using inverse kinematics. Originally developed as an ad hoc motion-capture tool for character animation.

VR Conductor · Under Development

Part VR rhythm game and part DAW interaction toolkit. Uses hand gestures to control MIDI playback, including tempo, intensity and individual orchestral parts.

3D Mesh Enhancement · IET Published Research

An MSc project developed at Cranfield University using digital signal processing and image-processing techniques to enhance 3D meshes from colour-texture information. The work was subsequently published in an IET journal.

About Me

I am a Senior Software Engineer with over two decades of professional experience delivering complex, high-reliability software systems across defence, simulation, and advanced research domains. I am a full-stack engineer with deep expertise spanning multiple operating systems, programming languages, and architectural and design methodologies, and I have a consistent record of translating complex requirements into robust, maintainable solutions.

My technical background includes extensive hands-on experience in C, C++, C#, Python, Fortran, Pascal, and x86 assembly, alongside long-term work in Unity, real-time 3D, VR/XR, and immersive simulation technologies, including Oculus platforms. I have designed and delivered software for PC, Android, Linux, and UNIX environments, often operating at the intersection of software and hardware to meet demanding performance and reliability constraints.

I am an IET-published author in the field of Digital Signal and Image Processing and have contributed to both applied research and operational systems. In parallel with my industry work, I have designed and delivered MSc-level curricula, lectured at postgraduate level, supervised MSc dissertations, and served as a Viva examiner, reflecting a strong commitment to technical rigor, mentorship, and knowledge transfer.

Throughout my career, I have led and contributed to the development of mission-critical systems in areas including defence, command and control (C2), robotics, war-gaming, modelling and simulation, and virtual reality. I have worked with and for organisations such as the Royal School of Signals (UK Ministry of Defence), the NATO C3 Agency, and Cranfield University.

I am currently an independent software consultant and developer, providing expert-level technical leadership, system design, and hands-on development for clients requiring deep domain knowledge, architectural insight, and dependable delivery of complex software solutions.
