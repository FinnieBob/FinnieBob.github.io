Realtime 3D Pose Estimation & Mapping from Video

This project used Unity and the Barracuda Neural Network inference library to provide realtime pose estimation from 2D video. The 2D skeleton was then transformed into a 3D model using inverse kinematics.
This was originally designed as an ad hoc motion capture tool to create character animations for other projects

Project 'THISTLE'. Command & Control (C2) Software Suite. British MoD, NATO C3A

During my time at the Microprocessor Applications Group at Cranfield University, I was a lead developer and engineer on many projects for the British MoD and NATO. Main activity was the development of Command and Control, Communications Planning and Wargaming software tools with a focus on rapid prototyping. An broad suite of C3 tools, developed between 1994-2010, these were used extensively both in training and military exercise and deployed live in theatre (Bosnia & Afghanistan)

VR Conductor (Under Development)

Part VR Rhythm Game, part DAW interaction toolkit. This project is both a rhythm game where the player takes on the role of an orchestra conductor and also a toolkit to control a digital audio workstation (DAW). Using common hand gestures, the conductor can affect the arrangement and playback of MIDI songs, from changing the tempo and intensity of the piece, to the fine tuning and voicing of individual members of the orchestra.

One of the MSc projects I developed whilst at Cranfield University. This was published in the IET journal and focuses on using DSP techniques and image processing to enhance 3D meshes from colour texture information.

CADUCEUS (EW Training & Simulation Suite)

Developed in conjunction with The Royal School of Signals (RSS) This software training simulation was developed during my time at Cranfield University. It utilised the SPECTRA/THISTLE project. It was used by training staff to emulate a series of EW transmissions to be detected by students. The exercise DS would provide students with a list of Electronic Warfare assets that can be deployed in specified areas of responsibility. To aid the deployment of assets the tool has a number of communications testing functions that can be used to determine the optimum location for each asset. Once deployment is complete, the scenario will run in real time enabling students to build-up a picture of troop movements based on detections made. To aid construction of the movement picture, students within each cell can use the system to share information

About Me

I am a Senior Software Engineer with over three decades of professional experience delivering complex, high-reliability software systems across defence, simulation, and advanced research domains. I am a full-stack engineer with deep expertise spanning multiple operating systems, programming languages, and architectural and design methodologies, and I have a consistent record of translating complex requirements into robust, maintainable solutions.
My technical background includes extensive hands-on experience in C, C++, C#, Python, Fortran, Pascal, and x86 assembly, alongside long-term work in Unity, real-time 3D, VR/XR, and immersive simulation technologies, including Oculus platforms. I have designed and delivered software for PC, Android, Linux, and UNIX environments, often operating at the intersection of software and hardware to meet demanding performance and reliability constraints.
I am an IET-published author in the field of Digital Signal and Image Processing and have contributed to both applied research and operational systems. In parallel with my industry work, I have designed and delivered MSc-level curricula, lectured at postgraduate level, supervised MSc dissertations, and served as a Viva examiner, reflecting a strong commitment to technical rigor, mentorship, and knowledge transfer.
Throughout my career, I have led and contributed to the development of mission-critical systems in areas including defence, command and control (C2), robotics, war-gaming, modelling and simulation, and virtual reality. I have worked with and for organisations such as the Royal School of Signals (UK Ministry of Defence), the NATO C3 Agency, and Cranfield University.
I am currently an independent software consultant and developer, providing expert-level technical leadership, system design, and hands-on development for clients requiring deep domain knowledge, architectural insight, and dependable delivery of complex software solutions..
